# Code of conduct

schemagate adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, for everyone taking part in its issues, pull requests,
discussions and any other project space.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, criticise ideas rather than people,
and do not harass anyone. The full text at the link above is the one that applies.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub's
private reporting form,
<https://github.com/ashishsinha1602/schemagate/security/advisories/new>,
with a title starting "Conduct:". It reaches only the maintainer. Reports are
answered within 7 days and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and may respond, as the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines describe, with a correction, a warning, a temporary ban,
or a permanent ban, depending on what happened.
