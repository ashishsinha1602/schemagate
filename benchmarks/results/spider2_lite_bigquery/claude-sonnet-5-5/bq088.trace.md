## Question
Please calculate the average levels of anxiety and depression symptoms from the weekly country data for the United States during the periods from January 1, 2019, to January 1, 2020, and from January 1, 2020, to January 1, 2021. Then, compute the percentage increase in these average symptom levels from the 2019 period to the 2020 period.

## Attempt 1
```sql
WITH periods AS (
  SELECT
    AVG(IF(date >= DATE '2019-01-01' AND date < DATE '2020-01-01', SAFE_CAST(symptom_anxiety AS FLOAT64), NULL)) AS avg_anxiety_2019,
    AVG(IF(date >= DATE '2020-01-01' AND date < DATE '2021-01-01', SAFE_CAST(symptom_anxiety AS FLOAT64), NULL)) AS avg_anxiety_2020,
    AVG(IF(date >= DATE '2019-01-01' AND date < DATE '2020-01-01', SAFE_CAST(symptom_depression AS FLOAT64), NULL)) AS avg_depression_2019,
    AVG(IF(date >= DATE '2020-01-01' AND date < DATE '2021-01-01', SAFE_CAST(symptom_depression AS FLOAT64), NULL)) AS avg_depression_2020
  FROM `bigquery-public-data.covid19_symptom_search.symptom_search_country_weekly`
  WHERE country_region = 'United States'
    AND date >= DATE '2019-01-01' AND date < DATE '2021-01-01'
)
SELECT
  avg_anxiety_2019,
  avg_anxiety_2020,
  SAFE_DIVIDE(avg_anxiety_2020 - avg_anxiety_2019, avg_anxiety_2019) * 100 AS anxiety_pct_increase,
  avg_depression_2019,
  avg_depression_2020,
  SAFE_DIVIDE(avg_depression_2020 - avg_depression_2019, avg_depression_2019) * 100 AS depression_pct_increase
FROM periods
```
