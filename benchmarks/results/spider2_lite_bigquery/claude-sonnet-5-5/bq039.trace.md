## Question
Find the top 10 taxi trips in New York City between July 1 and July 7, 2016 (ensuring both pickup and dropoff times fall within these dates) where the passenger count is greater than five, the trip distance is at least ten miles, and there are no negative fare-related amounts (including tip, tolls, mta tax, fare, and total costs). Exclude any trips where the dropoff time is not strictly after the pickup time, then sort the results by total fare amount in descending order. Finally, display each trip’s pickup zone, dropoff zone, trip duration in seconds, driving speed in miles per hour, and tip rate as a percentage of the total fare amount.

    y.dropoff_location_id,
    y.trip_distance,
    y.tip_amount,
    y.total_amount,
    TIMESTAMP_DIFF(y.dropoff_datetime, y.pickup_datetime, SECOND) AS trip_duration_seconds

    AND y.dropoff_datetime >= TIMESTAMP('2016-07-01')
    AND y.dropoff_datetime < TIMESTAMP('2016-07-08')
    AND y.dropoff_datetime > y.pickup_datetime
    AND y.passenger_count > 5
    AND y.trip_distance >= 10
    AND y.tip_amount >= 0
    AND y.tolls_amount >= 0
    AND y.mta_tax >= 0
    AND y.fare_amount >= 0
    AND y.total_amount >= 0
)
SELECT
  pz.zone_name AS pickup_zone,
  dz.zone_name AS dropoff_zone,
  t.trip_duration_seconds,
  SAFE_DIVIDE(t.trip_distance, t.trip_duration_seconds / 3600) AS driving_speed_mph,
  SAFE_DIVIDE(t.tip_amount, t.total_amount) * 100 AS tip_rate_percent
FROM t
LEFT JOIN `bigquery-public-data.new_york_taxi_trips.taxi_zone_geom` AS pz
  ON t.pickup_location_id = pz.zone_id
LEFT JOIN `bigquery-public-data.new_york_taxi_trips.taxi_zone_geom` AS dz
  ON t.dropoff_location_id = dz.zone_id
ORDER BY t.total_amount DESC
LIMIT 10
