## Question
Which three companies had the largest increase in trip numbers between two consecutive months in 2018?

## Attempt 1
```sql
WITH monthly AS (
  SELECT
    company,
    DATE_TRUNC(DATE(trip_start_timestamp), MONTH) AS month,
    COUNT(*) AS trips
  FROM `bigquery-public-data.chicago_taxi_trips.taxi_trips`
  WHERE trip_start_timestamp >= TIMESTAMP('2018-01-01')
    AND trip_start_timestamp < TIMESTAMP('2019-01-01')
  GROUP BY company, month
),
diffs AS (
  SELECT
    company,
    month,
    trips - LAG(trips) OVER (PARTITION BY company ORDER BY month) AS increase
  FROM monthly
)
SELECT company
FROM diffs
WHERE increase IS NOT NULL
GROUP BY company
ORDER BY MAX(increase) DESC
LIMIT 3
```
